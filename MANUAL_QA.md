Browser-control tooling is unavailable in this session. The nine uploaded photographs have been inspected locally and integrated. These steps still need a real browser: file inspection and containment rules do not establish actual layout, contrast, motion or overflow quality.

3. Hero: wait at least 11 seconds for the active slide/indicator to change. Inspect the 1600ms mask/crossfade and subtle scale with the installed photographs. Press Pause and wait 22 seconds: no advance. Press Play: advancement resumes. Select a slide, use previous/next, focus a CTA or touch the hero: automatic advancement stops. On mobile, close remains visible. One-photo records must not pretend to have alternate views; controls are explicit. Test zoom with the installed photographs. ## Exhibition / Garment Gallery acceptance

At 390×844, 768×1024 and 1440×900 (and a 360px overflow check):

1. Read descriptions at normal zoom. Confirm 17–18px descriptive type, secondary text at least 14px, comfortable widths, 1.6–1.75 line height and no clipped hints/category labels. Check solid hero, fabric caption and contact surfaces; independently audit rendered contrast. Declared colour-pair calculations are not a browser audit.
2. Inspect the face-free crops: collar/embroidery, sleeves and photographed silhouette remain useful, and no face is cut through the middle. The cap image is deliberately a fabric-pattern detail, not a full product shot. The sand kaftan's original tag stays visible.
3. Hero: allow about 10.6 seconds between changes (1.6-second transition plus 9-second hold). Pause, wait at least 22 seconds, then Play. Manual slide selection, CTA focus, document keyboard/pointer interaction, wheel scrolling and tab hiding must stop automatic advance. Buttons work during transitions.
4. Scroll to The Garment Gallery. Verify the central framed garment is large, neighbours recessed and shadows restrained. Select a neighbour or use Previous/Next repeatedly during motion: state changes immediately without locking controls. Captions remain stationary and never tilt. Explore this look opens the selected reference and Escape/Close restores focus.
5. Keyboard: tab to the gallery controls/panels; use ArrowLeft/Right, Home/End and Enter. Focus remains visible. Screen-reader caption updates are polite; there is no automatic gallery rotation.
6. Touch: swipe horizontally on the image area. One look advances and no incidental modal opens. Vertical drags continue native page scrolling. Explicit mobile buttons remain visible without horizontal page overflow; neighbours may intentionally be partially recessed/clipped within the stage.
7. Desktop pointer: only the central photographic panel responds gently, limited to 4 degrees per axis; leaving resets it. Neither caption nor controls tilt. Do not infer that another side of the garment is being rendered.
8. Emulate reduced motion and repeat. No slideshow autoplay, parallax, spatial gallery movement or tilt; manual gallery selection still works and hidden neighbours are excluded from keyboard focus. Toggle reduced motion while a hero slide is active and verify automatic movement stops.
9. Regenerate/open the standalone HTML and repeat Gallery selection/details, slow hero controls, Skip/Scroll/Atelier, filtering and WhatsApp drafts. Capture a short recording of transitions as well as screenshots; neither was available in this session.
